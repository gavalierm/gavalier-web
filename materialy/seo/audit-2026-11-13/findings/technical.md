# Technical SEO + Performance + Images: gavalier.sk (audit 2026-11-13)
Skore: Technical 80/100, Performance 62/100 (odhad zo zdrojov, nie lab), Images 58/100.
Live HTML == zdroj v repe (diff identicky). Vsetkych 8 URL zo sitemap = 200, canonical = vlastna URL, lang="sk", viewport OK, unikatne title/description/H1.

## Findings
1. HIGH, Security: ziadne bezpecnostne hlavicky. curl -I na / a podstranky: chyba Strict-Transport-Security, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options/frame-ancestors, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy, CSP. Oprava: subor `_headers` v koreni (Cloudflare static assets) s `/*` + HSTS (max-age=31536000), nosniff, Referrer-Policy strict-origin-when-cross-origin, frame-ancestors 'self'.
2. HIGH, Perf/Images (LCP): hero obrazok assets/gavo_edited.png = 1 340 165 B PNG 1144x1086 (index.html:492, fetchpriority=high, alt="" dekorativny). Je to LCP kandidat; ~1,3 MB na mobile. Oprava: AVIF/WebP (~60-120 KB), srcset 600/1144, zachovat width/height. Rovnako gavo_colored_eye.png 279 KB (index.html:444, 932x228, na kazdej stranke v hlavicke) -> WebP/SVG, zobrazuje sa ~maly. og_image.png 272 KB (1212x716, nie 1200x630) -> JPG ~80 KB, 1200x630.
3. MEDIUM, Perf: Cache-Control `public, max-age=0, must-revalidate` na vsetkych assetoch (CSS/JS/obrazky; curl -I). Verzie `?v=` sa nevyuzivaju. Oprava: `_headers` pre /assets/*, /css/*, /js/* `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` (pri zmene menit ?v= alebo nazov).
4. MEDIUM, Perf: index.html nacitava 15 render-blocking CSS suborov (index.html:59-73, spolu ~76 KB nezlozene) + Google Fonts z externej domeny (index.html:56). Oprava: zlucit/minifikovat CSS do 1 suboru, self-host Manrope woff2 s preload (odstrani 2 spojenia a blokujuci request). JS je defer (OK).
5. MEDIUM, CLS: klientske loga (index.html:920-1288, ~75 <img>) nemaju width/height; iba loading=lazy. Min-height 100px v clients-logos.css, ale loga v marquee mozu posunut layout. Oprava: width/height na kazdy <img>. Duplikaty loga s alt="" pre marquee su spravne (dekorativne).
6. LOW, Crawlability: robots.txt:`Crawl-delay: 1` (Google ignoruje, Bing berie); dlhy zoznam rovnakych `Allow: /` je zbytocny, pokrýva ho `User-agent: *`. Sitemap deklarovana a validna (sitemap_discovery.py: valid urlset, 200). Nie je chyba, len redundancia. Pozn.: Google-Extended/anthropic-ai/Claude-Web su povolene zamerne.
7. LOW, Sitemap: vsetky lastmod su 2026-11-11 (rovnaky den = bulk), Google ich moze ignorovat. Oprava: nastavovat lastmod len pri realnej zmene obsahu. Image sitemap obsahuje len gavo_edited.png s title obsahujucim "\" (sitemap.xml: "Audio \ Video").
8. LOW, 404: /neexistuje vracia HTTP 404, ale prazdne telo (0 B), ziadna vlastna 404.html. Oprava: pridat 404.html v koreni a `"not_found_handling": "404-page"` v wrangler.jsonc assets.
9. LOW, URL/redirects: /blog -> 307 (nie 301) na /blog/; /index.html -> 307 na /; /AI-agenti/ -> 307. 307 je predvolene spravanie Cloudflare auto-trailing-slash; Google ho spracuje ako docasne presmerovanie, kanonicke odkazy v HTML su uz s lomkou, takze vplyv je maly. Interne odkazy overit, aby nesli bez lomky.
10. LOW, Mobile/PWA: manifest.json ma ikonu len SVG s purpose "any maskable" (nema PNG 192/512 -> instalovatelnost slabsia); index.html odkazuje relativne (`css/`, `assets/`) kym podstranky absolutne (`/css/`) - funguje, ale nekonzistentne. Chyba favicon.ico (404; prehliadace bezne beru SVG, Google favicon z <link>).
